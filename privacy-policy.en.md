# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

[Operator name] ("we", "us") has established this Privacy Policy to explain how we handle information in the iOS app "Itadakimono" (the "App").

## 1. Our approach

The App helps you record the gifts you receive with photos so that you don't forget to return the favor. The App has no account registration, and everything you record is stored only on your device. We never receive or see the photos or details you record.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

The following information you record in the App is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party:

- Photos of gifts you received and gifts you gave in return
- Item names, amounts, dates received, and details of what you gave in return
- Names of the people who gave you gifts, their icons or photos, and short notes about them
- App settings such as reminder dates, notification time, and display preferences

## 3. Access to device features

The App uses the following features only with your permission. You can still use the rest of the App if you don't grant permission.

| Feature | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Camera | To take photos of gifts you received or gave |
| Photo library | To choose photos for your records (the selected photos are copied and stored inside the App) |
| Notifications | To remind you about returning gifts. Notifications are scheduled on your device, and no information is sent outside your device for them |

## 4. Information collected by third-party services

The App uses the third-party services below. These services may collect information directly from your device in accordance with their own privacy policies. In no case is the information you record in the App (described in Section 2) sent to them.

### (1) RevenueCat (managing paid plan purchases)

We use services provided by RevenueCat, Inc. to check and manage the status of paid plan (Premium) purchases. The following information may be collected:

- An anonymous user ID generated automatically by the App
- Purchase history, such as the products purchased, purchase date and time, and expiration date
- Technical information such as device and OS type, app version, and IP address

Payments are processed by Apple. We never receive payment information such as credit card numbers.

- RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

### (2) Google AdMob (serving ads)

We use Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to serve the ads shown when you raise the limits of the free plan. Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one. The following information may be collected:

- Technical information such as device and OS type and IP address
- Information about your interactions with ads, such as views and taps
- The advertising identifier, if allowed in your device settings

This information is used to serve ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud. You can limit the use of the advertising identifier in your device's Settings.

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 5. Transfer of information to third parties outside Japan

The providers of the services in Section 4 are located in the United States and may process the collected information on servers outside Japan. For the measures each provider takes to protect information, please see their privacy policies.

## 6. How we use information

We use information obtained through the third-party services in Section 4 only for the following purposes:

- To provide the paid plan and to verify and restore purchases
- To serve ads and to provide the feature that raises the free plan limits
- To fix problems and improve the App
- To respond to your inquiries

## 7. Sharing with third parties

We do not share or sell your information to third parties without your consent, except as required by law.

## 8. Deleting your information

- You can delete everything you have recorded at any time from "Settings" → "Delete all data" in the App.
- If you delete the App from your device, the data on your device will also be lost. Because we do not store this data, it cannot be recovered.
- If you would like information held by the third-party services to be deleted, please contact us using the information in Section 10.
